# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical evidence while remaining respectful.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Clearly distinguish measurements, estimates, and future plans.
- Respect dependency licenses, data provenance, and attribution.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes openly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation,
personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, and
other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, and
other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method
on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as
confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>
